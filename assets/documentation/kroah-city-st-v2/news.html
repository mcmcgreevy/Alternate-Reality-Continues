News



This reverse engineering work was done on the Atari ST version of Alternate Reality: "The City" (v2.0).

The initial game was released on Atari 8-bit but the game engine in the City (16-bit) is closer to Alternate Reality: "The Dungeon" (8-bit).

This leads to many differences between both versions of the City and I hope this work will help finding them.


Jim Norris has put online a disassembly project of Alternate Reality: "The City" (8-bit) to share disassembly information online.

Thanks to Acrin, Hicksman, Robert and Wrathchild for their help and support!
