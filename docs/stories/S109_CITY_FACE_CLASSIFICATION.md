# S109 — Complete City face evidence classifications

## Outcome
Give every City face a reviewed, evidence-linked classification so incomplete historical verification is visible and actionable.

## Scope and acceptance
- Review all 4096 cells / 16384 directed faces; preserve separate canonical-image, candidate-data and runtime-semantic confidence.
- Replace the remaining 16240 unverified/uncompared placeholders with an actual reviewed disposition or an explicitly evidenced limitation. Preserve/review the 144 examined-but-image-unresolved cases.
- Allowed conclusions include verified match, discrepancy, not resolved by image and outside supported interpretation. Numeric equality alone is not a fidelity pass.
- Every unresolved classification records the source limitation, player-facing significance, next evidence needed and owning story; no bulk relabeling to manufacture completion.
- Reconcile unique face coverage, counts and reverse-face relationships against existing ledgers. Never assume opposite faces are reciprocal.
- Link actionable geometry to S108 or S111 and appearance to S110. Establishment/scenario investigations retain their existing owners.
- Produce an audit summary distinguishing reviewed coverage from verified historical behavior.

## Boundaries
Research and classification only; no map or rendering replacement. Finishing classification does not certify historically accurate behavior at unresolved faces.
