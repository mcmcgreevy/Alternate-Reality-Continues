# First Excursion

Purpose: test establishment menus, street navigation and the shared character across the City/Dungeon boundary. This is a playtest exercise, not a recovered original quest. There is no combat, reward or new map geometry.

Start a fresh run with Play.cmd. Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the guide enabled.

1. In The Tavern, press **2** to read the briefing. Try movement keys: the menu should remain in place without advancing the clock. Lyrics and music continue.
2. Press **2** to return, then **1** to order food/drink. Choose **1** for Chili and **Y** to confirm (or N to cancel without spending). Your 8 gold becomes 7 gold, 9 silver, 2 copper; time advances to 18:15. Food reduces hunger without healing. The meal is optional.
3. Press **5** to return to the tavern choices, then **3** to leave. You should appear just north of the tavern; music stops and the clock advances one minute. Follow the guide with W/S, A/D and E; Tab shows the route. Some boundaries are secret or one-way.
4. At the entrance, face east and press **E**. You arrive in the Dungeon facing west, with the same health, gold and inventory; the crossing costs five minutes.
5. Step west once. Press **F5**, take note of position, time and possessions, move or wait, then **F9**. The saved state should return. F5 replaces your current original-route save.
6. Face east and step back to the arrival point. Press **E** to return to the City. Follow the return guide; one-way boundaries mean it takes a different route.
7. Enter The Tavern. Its menu and song should return automatically, and the objective should read complete. Coins remain 7 gold, 9 silver, 2 copper if you bought only the Chili; inventory remains Worn sword and Bread. Without extra movement or waits, the Chili plus corridor excursion brings the clock to 21:29. Open the menu again: the 21:00 selection differs from 18:00.

Things to report: a confusing exit or interaction, a guide that contradicts movement, a door that behaves differently from its displayed side, a save that loses state, or music that continues in the street. Also note whether the tavern feels like an establishment screen rather than a room to explore.

Known limits: the excursion, menu rotation schedule and need/timing formulas remain remake scaffolding. Food/water/provision services have historical evidence; most prices remain secondary-source values pending original-runtime verification. See [tavern notes](TAVERN.md). Original events, encounters and exact door checks remain future work.
