## What finishing S05 means

The split gives S05 a bounded inventory and identity outcome. Most source collection already exists; remaining work is consolidation, consistency checking, unresolved-identity ownership and final acceptance, with targeted research only where that audit exposes a gap.

1. Reconcile all 67 canonical legend entries, 66 observed markers, 463 numbered cells and 73 labeled components with the current 81 location components. Distinguish these counts rather than treating them as interchangeable.
2. Verify a stable identity record for every occurrence, with category, marker, coordinates/footprint, source locator, confidence, service-state identity where known and the existing owning issue.
3. Explicitly review absent Trade Winds, both Da Place entrances and shared shop state, inaccessible duplicates, reused Arena labels, unnumbered/mixed gate and approach cells, and both Dungeon markers. No marker-only service identity or invented location.
4. Preserve the approved Tail of the Dog/Grimthorn, music and northern cross-edition travel adaptations. Link each remaining identity/dispatch uncertainty to the responsible story with the evidence needed to resolve it.
5. Verify inventory counts, uniqueness, issue links and ownership coverage. Publish a final acceptance review against the retained S05 criteria, then close the story only if that review passes.

S05 closure does not certify complete historical map fidelity or complete F05. S109 owns remaining face classifications, S110 owns visual reconciliation and S111 owns held passage contracts. These obligations remain open and visible. Passing work to them must follow the agreed split, not silently remove an unresolved inventory requirement.

## Readiness and work sequence

Start with S05 inventory acceptance. If a missing identity affects one of S108's exact correction boundaries, resolve that specific dependency before editing it; unrelated face/scenario research does not block the eight-face correction set.

Next settle S108's geometry/save revision policy, including arbitrary legacy door keys and the obsolete keys `city:26,34:2` and `city:26,35:0`. Preserve positions and unrelated door state. New door faces default closed unless separately justified. Keep all 79 service encodings and held Arena/Palace approaches unchanged.

Then implement and validate S108. Use the integrated movement baseline, with S06's remaining checks run against the same final build. S06 is acceptance and demonstrated gap correction, not a rewrite. Six walking units per tile remains provisional calibration; record measured evidence or obtain an explicit adaptation decision before closure. Positive movement feedback establishes usability, not historical timing fidelity.

Aim for a focused testable build by midweek, with the latter part reserved for player feedback, fixes and closeout. This is a target, not a guaranteed date. If user testing is delayed, continue documentation and acceptance research without treating silence as approval.

## Integrated acceptance

- S05: final inventory and issue-ownership audit passes, and its acceptance review records both verified findings and remaining owned uncertainties.
- S108: test both directions at all four physical boundaries; stale open-door keys cannot bypass the wall. Save/load on both sides and supported legacy migration preserve valid journeys without stranding players.
- S108: confirm expected exits from newly accessible cells, both tavern cells and south exit, northern City/Dungeon round trip and no new southeast crossing.
- S06: verify 30/60/144 FPS, delayed frames, Speed thresholds, effect expiry during travel, pause/resume, collision, directional passages, transitions and no held-input carryover after restore. Explain intentional input-scheduling differences.
- Preserve accepted Dungeon darkness, visible SPD and existing persistence/time behavior. Run relevant regression suites and one short manual route/control script. Record player acceptance before merging gameplay changes.

## Capacity tradeoff

S05 completion replaces S07 readiness in the core forecast. Character-creation implementation is no longer a stretch promise. F08's bounded feature review is optional only after the three core stories are secure; S07 refinement can follow if capacity remains. Neither is required to meet this sprint goal.

This keeps the forecast at one inventory closeout plus two bounded implementation/acceptance outcomes. Last sprint supports that as a reasonable estimate, but does not supply reliable story points or a precise daily usage budget. Reserve capacity for migration and playtest findings rather than filling the week with more stories.

If core work expands, drop optional F08/S07 work first. If the three-story forecast becomes unrealistic, report the specific blocker and reforecast explicitly; do not quietly drop S05 or relax its acceptance. S109–S111, Arena/Palace scenarios, location services, weather, metabolism and items are outside this sprint forecast.
